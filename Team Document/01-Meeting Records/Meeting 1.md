# Meeting 1: Domain Selection

**Goal:** The primary objective of this inaugural session was to evaluate potential project directions, deliberate on feasible research scopes, and formally finalize the overarching thematic domain for our semester project through open discussion and a democratic team vote.  
  
**Decisions:** During the meeting, the group conducted a comprehensive evaluation of two preliminary proposals: "enhancing networking through common interests" versus "combating game addiction." After thoroughly weighing the societal impact, potential technical avenues, and collective enthusiasm for each topic, the team held a conclusive vote and officially selected **combating game addiction** as our central focus. Although networking presented practical value, the team agreed that excessive gameplay presents richer behavioral challenges and deeper design possibilities. Furthermore, members recognized that game engagement intersects heavily with contemporary mobile technology and modern communication habits, making it an intellectually engaging problem space that aligns closely with our academic curriculum.  
  
**Future Plans:** Moving forward, each team member is tasked with independently reviewing academic literature and scholarly articles pertinent to gaming habits, problematic use, and digital well-being. Everyone will identify recurring themes, existing intervention technologies, and potential gaps in the literature. The team will then reconvene to synthesize these individual findings and brainstorm concrete design spaces for upcoming phases.
