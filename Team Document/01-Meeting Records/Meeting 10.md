## Meeting 10: Exhibition Preparation & Task Allocation



**Goal:** The purpose of this meeting was to coordinate the final preparation for the DECO3500 exhibition, clarify individual responsibilities, and ensure that the prototype, visual materials, testing, and project documentation would be ready for presentation.

**Decisions:** The team confirmed the following division of work for the period before the exhibition:

| Team Member | Responsibility           | Main Tasks                                                   |
| ----------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Kiki**    | **Code adjustments**     | Continue refining Pally's functionality and address feasible prototype issues before the exhibition. |
