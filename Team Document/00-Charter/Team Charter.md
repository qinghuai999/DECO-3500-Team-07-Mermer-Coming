## Communication Plan ##
The team will use WeChat as the main channel for daily communication, project updates, and general discussions. GitHub will serve as the primary platform for task development, issue tracking, technical discussions, source-code management, and code review. The GitHub repository will be considered the authoritative version of the project. Important decisions that affect the project's direction, tasks, deadlines, design, or development should be communicated to all team members and documented in meeting notes or a shared project document for future reference.
Team members are expected to respond to messages within one working day under normal circumstances. When an issue is urgent, its urgency should be communicated clearly. Members should also notify the team as early as possible if they are unable to attend a meeting, encounter scheduling conflicts, or expect delays in completing their assigned tasks.
The team will normally hold a meeting every Saturday from 4:00 pm to 6:00 pm, either offline or through Zoom. These meetings will focus on reviewing weekly progress, identifying and discussing design or development issues, making necessary project decisions, and allocating tasks for the following week. With the agreement of all team members, meetings may be recorded. A brief meeting summary should include key decisions, assigned tasks, deadlines, and identified risks. Meeting notes and recordings will be stored in the shared team space. Members are expected to attend meetings on time and provide updates on their progress. If a member cannot attend due to a scheduling conflict, they should notify the team in advance and arrange an alternative time to discuss their progress.
For development work, team members must follow Git practices that maintain project stability, traceability, and accountability. The main branch should contain only the most stable version of the project, and substantial changes must not be committed directly to it. Instead, each feature, bug fix, or major task should normally be developed in a separate branch created from the latest stable version. Before starting the work, the corresponding GitHub Issue should be confirmed to ensure that the task has a clearly defined scope and an assigned owner.
Conflict Resolution Plan
All major disagreements and important decisions must be settled during team meetings, where every member can share their views. If the group cannot reach an agreement after the first discussion, members should consider alternative solutions before the meeting ends to avoid delays. Each team member will explain their ideas, after which the team will discuss and vote on the final decision.

Every team member is expected to contribute fairly to the project. Members are required to attend scheduled meetings and complete assigned work on time. If a member cannot attend a meeting or faces issues that prevent them from finishing their work, they should notify the team as soon as possible. The team will check progress regularly and re‑assign tasks if needed to keep workloads balanced.

Any concerns regarding missed meetings, overdue tasks, communication problems, work quality or unequal contributions should first be raised politely with the involved member and discussed by the whole team. The team will agree on improvement steps and record these with a review date. If the problem cannot be resolved, relevant evidence will be collected and the issue escalated to the teaching team.

## Team Charter Agreement ##
The team has discussed and approved the expectations set out in this Team Charter. All members recognize that effective teamwork requires communication, accountability, professionalism and active contribution. The team will raise concerns in a timely manner and follow this charter to resolve team issues.
