## Meeting 9: User Testing Review & Findings Analysis



**Goal:** The primary objective of this meeting was to review the completed user testing of Pally, compare group interactions with and without the prototype, and identify the main findings that should guide further development. The team also clarified each member's contribution to the evaluation stage.

**Decisions:** We recruited **three participants** to test the prototype in multiplayer gaming scenarios. The evaluation compared **Session A (Discord interaction without Pally)** with **Session B (interaction using Pally)**, followed by participant interviews. **Ping, Arthur, and Jeric** observed the sessions and documented group interaction using the **Groupware Heuristics** framework. **Elsie** reviewed the testing evidence and interview responses, then organised the findings into a thematic analysis. Meanwhile, **Kiki** continued refining the bot's code and functionality.

The analysis identified four main themes:

1. **Decision-making power:** More vocal members tended to influence decisions in the baseline session. **Random Pick** gave a quieter member a temporary opportunity to decide, although existing group influence did not disappear.
2. **Anonymous expression:** **Trait Picker** made feedback easier for some participants, but members of a familiar group could still sometimes infer who had submitted a comment. Randomly selected evaluation targets were not always relevant to what players wanted to say.
3. **Leaving as negotiation:** **Excuse Generator** made the intention to leave more visible, but teammates could still ask the player to continue. It functioned more as a playful exit signal than a guaranteed way to leave.
4. **Relationship context:** Participants differed in whether the tools felt more useful with close friends or less familiar teammates, showing that existing group norms influenced the experience.

Elsie's analysis suggested that Pally's most promising role is as a **neutral social mediator**: it can change how players communicate, give feedback, and make decisions, but it does not automatically remove social pressure or unequal influence.

**Future Plans:** The team will use the findings to guide prototype refinement. **Kiki** will continue code adjustments, focusing on functionality and interaction issues raised during evaluation. The observers' records and **Elsie's** thematic analysis will be kept in the GitHub documentation to support design decisions. We will then prepare for final testing and the exhibition.
