### Meeting 8: User Testing Protocol & Evaluation Framework

* **Goal:** 
  The primary objective of this session was to establish the comprehensive user testing protocol and evaluation standards for our product, "Pally." Following the preliminary completion of our interactive prototype, the team aimed to design realistic testing scenarios and follow-up interview guides focused predominantly on gathering rich qualitative feedback.

* **Decisions:** 
  Based on recommendations from our tutor and the collaborative, multi-user nature of our project, the team decided to adopt the **Groupware Heuristics** framework to guide our evaluation design. Because Groupware Heuristics is specifically formulated to evaluate shared groupware applications across eight core dimensions of collaboration, it enables us to examine whether and how effectively the system facilitates natural group interactions, coordination, and social dynamics. Grounded in this framework, we finalized the testing protocol:
  * **Three Simulated Gaming Scenarios:** We constructed three distinct role-playing scenarios tailored to test Pally's three core features under realistic pressure:
    1. *Decision Conflicts:* Navigating disagreements over group choices using the randomized decision tool versus standard group discussion.
    2. *Ignored Individual Voices:* Managing situations where a player's opinions or feedback are overlooked, evaluating the anonymous trait picker and title generator against unassisted post-game interaction.
    3. *Desire to Exit the Session:* Testing the humorous excuse generator when a player wants to leave an ongoing session, compared to attempting to quit unassisted under peer pressure.
  * **Comparative Assessment:** Each scenario will explicitly evaluate user behavior and group dynamics in two conditions: with Pally versus without Pally, highlighting the behavioral shifts enabled by the system.
  * **Post-Test Semi-Structured Interviews:** Immediately following the scenario simulations, researchers will conduct in-depth debrief interviews. Inquiries will explore participants' overall experience, emotional comfort, perceived social friction, and their subjective impressions regarding the differences between using Pally and navigating social pressures unaided.

* **Future Plans:** 
  The team will execute the planned user evaluation sessions and synthesize the qualitative interview and observation data. Based on these findings, we will iteratively refine and polish the current prototype while exploring the implementation of secondary, nice-to-have features for subsequent iterations.
