# Meeting 7: Affinity Diagramming & Feature Ideation

**Goal:** 
  The primary goal of this meeting was to analyze the qualitative data from our second round of user interviews using an affinity diagram, validate various possible directions we concluded from researches and articles, and translate the core user pain points into three concrete, practical feature concepts for our upcoming product.  
  
**Decisions:** 
  Following the previous session where we identified three main friction points surrounding social compliance and peer pressure in multiplayer gaming groups, we conducted targeted interviews to explore these dynamics. The resulting affinity diagram confirmed that our initial assumptions aligned closely with genuine player experiences:  
  1. **Dominant Voices in Group Decisions:** Participants widely agreed that session choices (such as what to play, whom to invite, or when to stop) are typically dictated by more vocal or influential players, and other members usually just go along with them to avoid friction.  
  2. **Reluctance to Give Post-Game Feedback:** While some players are open to both giving and receiving post-match feedback, the majority withhold their honest thoughts because they fear hurting relationships or ruining the fun social atmosphere.  
  3. **High Discomfort with Saying "No" and Exiting:** Interviewees strongly felt that every player should have the right to decline invitations or leave freely. However, heavy peer pressure, squad dependency, and the awkward burden of having to justify their departure make saying "no" extremely uncomfortable.  
    
  To directly alleviate these social burdens and help players navigate these awkward situations effortlessly, the team brainstormed and agreed upon three core features:  
    
  **Randomized Decision Lottery (Equalizing Decision-Making Power)**  
  To address the implicit privilege and monopoly held by dominant group members, this feature uses a randomized draw/lottery system to select a player who gets to make the group decision. By distributing decision-making authority equally across all members, it eliminates the pressure of one person calling the shots.  
    
  **Anonymous "Trait Picker" & AI Title Generator (Low-Stakes Post-Game Feedback)**  
  To give players a safe, pressure-free way to comment on peer performance, the system randomly highlights a player after a match. Teammates then anonymously pick a noun or verb that describes that player's gameplay. An integrated AI then synthesizes these chosen words into a humorous, quirky, or lighthearted title/nickname. This turns potentially tense critique into a playful and non-threatening social interaction. 
    
  **Humorous Excuse Generator (Empowering Players to Say "No")**  
  To solve the dilemma of wanting to leave or reject a suggestion but being paralyzed by social guilt, this tool generates absurd, funny, and lighthearted excuses on the fly. Players can rely on these playful excuses to gracefully decline an invitation, step away from a session, or deflect unfair criticism without feeling awkward or damaging social bonds.  
    
**Future Plans:** 
  With the three core features clearly defined, the team's immediate next milestone is to build the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) and develop functional interactive prototypes. Once the MVP is assembled, we will run a round of user testing to evaluate usability, assess how naturally these features reduce social tension, and refine the interactions based on user feedback.
