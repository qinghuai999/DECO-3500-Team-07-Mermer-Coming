**Speaker 2:** Yes.

**Speaker 2:** I would probably just say, “Alright, I’m leaving.”

**Speaker 2:** If they disagree, then they disagree. I—

**Speaker 2:** I mean, I want to leave.

**Speaker 2:** Probably. But maybe not always.

**Speaker 2:** If they tried to convince me by saying, “Just one more game and then we’ll stop,” I might play one more. But after that one, I would probably actually leave.

**Speaker 2:** Very fair. I think Random Pick is probably the best of the three features.

**Speaker 2:** Sometimes there are a lot of disagreements about which map to choose or what to play, and nobody wants to give in. But if you have something like this, people might actually think, “Okay, let’s just go with this result.” So I think it feels quite fair.

**Speaker 1:** Overall, during the game, do you think the tools we provided changed the way you interacted with the group?

**Speaker 2:** In some ways.

**Speaker 1:** Can you explain a bit more?

**Speaker 2:** Like I said, I think the part about deciding whose choice to follow was really helpful.

**Speaker 2:** But for the leaving feature, I think when I am actually playing a game, I might easily miss that message. Or I might see it briefly and not really take it seriously.

**Speaker 2:** Also, with the anonymous evaluation, right now the system randomly chooses someone to evaluate. I would prefer being able to choose a specific person after a game and give feedback about them.

**Speaker 2:** But if the person selected randomly played normally and did not do anything especially good or especially bad, I probably would not really want to evaluate them. I think I would prefer this feature if I could choose the person myself.

**Speaker 2:** Actually, I think the tools might be even more useful with people I do not know well.

**Speaker 2:** But with people I do not know very well, for example if I wanted to leave, it might be harder to say it directly. If I used this tool, they would understand that I want to leave, and I would not need to explain so much.
